# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private
information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report anything that needs attention to **dan@dbhq.uk**. Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may block
accounts, where this is not followed.
