## What to go after

{question}

Worth attacking, in roughly this order:

1. **The premise.** Is the problem real, and is it the problem being solved?
2. **The economics.** Who pays, how much, and does that survive contact with
   what the alternatives cost - including doing nothing?
3. **The incumbents.** Who already does this, including the ones nobody
   counts: a spreadsheet, a script, an existing tool used slightly differently.
4. **The failure mode nobody has priced.** What breaks this two years in?
5. **What would have to be true** for this to work, and how likely each is.

Be specific. "The market may be competitive" is worth nothing; naming the
competitor and what it already gives away free is worth the whole run.
