# Request: your position on this, argued

{sole_reviewer}

Two parties are being asked the same question independently, then shown each
other's answers and asked to attack them. This is round {round}.

In round 0 you have not seen the other answer, and it has not seen yours. Give
the position you actually hold, with the reasoning that got you there - not a
survey of the options, and not a hedge that covers every case. A position that
can be argued against is worth more here than one that cannot.

In later rounds you will be shown the other position and asked to go at it
properly. Attack the reasoning, not the conclusion; find the assumption it rests
on and say whether it holds. Then revise your own position if the attack landed,
and say plainly what changed your mind. Changing your mind is a result, not a
loss.
